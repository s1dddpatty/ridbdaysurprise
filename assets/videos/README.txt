Put your family birthday video files here!
The expected filenames are:
1. mom.mp4         -> Video for 'Mom'
2. swayam.mp4      -> Video for 'Swayam'
3. mama_mami.mp4   -> Video for 'Mama Mami'
4. mayur.mp4       -> Video for 'Mayur'
5. siddhi.mp4      -> Video for 'Siddhi'
6. kaka_masi.mp4   -> Video for 'Kaka Masi'

When you drop any video with these names into this folder, it will automatically play inside the corresponding envelope!
